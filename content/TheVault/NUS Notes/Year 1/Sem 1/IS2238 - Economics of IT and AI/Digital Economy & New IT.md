# Digital Economy
---

A <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>digital economy</span>, is where production, distribution and consumption of economic goods and services are <span style='color:#f7b731'>all digitalized</span>.

As mentioned, the leveraging on IT on economic activity leads to economic growth.

**Results on digital economy**
- Rise of <span style='color:#f7b731'>E-commerce</span> for B2B and B2C transactions.
	Convenience and availability for customers 
- Increase usage and implementation of <span style='color:#f7b731'>digital payments</span> such as mobile payments, digital wallets, cryptocurrency

All of this is possible is because of the <span style='color:#f7b731'>infrastructure that supports the functionality</span> of it. 

## Digital Transformation

It is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>integration of digital technology into all areas of a business</span>, fundamentally changing how they operate and deliver value to customers, ultimately leading to innovation in the business model.

This transformation can be used to enhance:
- Customer experience
- Optimize Internal Processes
	Enable less hiring and reduce labor cost
- Increase Efficiency and Productivity
- Product new and innovative products
- Strategies for growth

However not all digital transformation is a success
- Consumer value
- Consumer culture
- No option given to the consumers

With digital transformation, companies can <span style='color:#f7b731'>stay competitive</span> in the market and can provide <span style='color:#f7b731'>more efficient solutions</span> for customers.

Some examples of companies who transformed digitally:

1) Starbucks

With their brick and mortar stores comes a application which allows users to order online to skip queues with the addition of gamification and personalized customer experience and product advertising.

2) Adidas

Creating an application which allows one to digitally try on their shoes. However its success is debatable, as the customer experience is different when trying shoes at a store as a user can feel how is it like to wear this shoe.

3) Airbnb

Streamline the accommodation booking process with customized suggestions based on the user's previous bookings.

4) TikTok

Allowing sellers to partner with content creators to promote their products to people who follow these creators. Using the influence content creators have to hook potential customers into buying a product. It also serves as another platform to sell their products as well with having low cost.

## Changes in the Digital Economy

IT <span style='color:#f7b731'>automates</span> many steps in business processes that were formerly performed manually (replacement of manual processes, robotic process automation (RPA)).

IT can enable decision makers to <span style='color:#f7b731'>make a more informed decision</span>.

# Supply Curve
---

It is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>is the quantity sellers are willing to sell at a particular price</span> during a particular period, assuming all other things are unchanged.

In general, when there are <span style='color:#f7b731'>many sellers of a good</span>, an <span style='color:#f7b731'>increase in price</span> results in an <span style='color:#f7b731'>increase in quantity</span>
supplied, and this relationship is often referred to as the <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>law of supply</span>.

A <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>supply shifter</span> can change the quantity of a good or service at each price. It includes prices of factors of production, technology, the number of sellers, and so on..

Electronics manufacturers continue to improve the quality of their products and lower the cost of production.
	According to the Bureau of Labor Statistics, the Consumer Price Index for computers declined 96 percent from 1997 to 2015. In the same period, the price index for televisions decreased 94 percent.

# Demand Curve
---

It is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>quantity buyers are willing and able to buy at a particular price</span> during a particular period, assuming all other things are unchanged.

The <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>law of demand</span> holds that, for virtually all goods and services, a <span style='color:#f7b731'>higher price leads to a reduction in quantity demanded</span> and a <span style='color:#f7b731'>lower price leads to an increase in quantity demanded</span>.

A  <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>demand shifter</span> can change the quantity of a good or service demanded at each price. When other variables change, the all other things unchanged condition behind the original demand curve no longer holds.

Although different goods and services will have different <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>demand shifters</span>, the demand shifters are likely to include consumer preferences, the quality of the goods, the prices of related goods and services, income, demographic characteristics, and so on.

This results in a worsening of mental health, sleep quality and well being as everyone is FOMO.

# Digital Divide
---

It is the gap between those who have access to technology and those who do not have.

Companies and consumers as well those who <span style='color:#f7b731'>does not have access to technology</span> will <span style='color:#f7b731'>not be able </span>to reap the <span style='color:#f7b731'>benefits</span> of economic prosperity that IT has brought along.

# Digitalized Crime
---

With the widespread usage of technology, it brought about digitalized crimes, which are crimes that are committed online. For instance hacking, ransom, scams, racial crimes, just to name a few.

Governments are finding it <span style='color:#f7b731'>difficult to fully eradicate </span>such crimes as the barrier of entry is relatively low. In addition, there are a lot of different types of crimes online, thus the resources available might not be enough to reduce all crime.

Without a good understanding on IT, it is difficult to find solutions to mitigate online crime.
