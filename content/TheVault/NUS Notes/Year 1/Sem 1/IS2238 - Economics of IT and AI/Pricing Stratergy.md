# Pricing
---

Setting a price for a product is not easy. It is a major factor which <span style='color:#f7b731'>determines the consumption of goods and services</span>. 

Price it too high and no one will buy it and price it too low, the profits will be minimal. Thus firms needs to find a price that <span style='color:#f7b731'>maximized profits while considering other factors</span>.

## Economic Value to Customer

Or EVC, is a measure on how much is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>perceived value of a product or service</span> is to a individual or a customer segment. This includes both <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>tangible</span> and <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>intangible</span> benefits.

**Tangible**
> Something which can be <span style='color:#f7b731'>quantified</span>

Example: When buying this shoe, a customer will feel more comfortable 

**Intangible**
> Something that <span style='color:#f7b731'>cannot be quantified</span> directly

Example: When buying this shoe, a customer might fell that he or she will look nicer

Another term for EVC is <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>utility</span>
> It is the total satisfaction or benefit of consuming a good or service

If the utility a product outweighs the value of the alternative, a customer will buy that product.

**How to calculate EVC**
EVC = Reference Value + Differentiation Value

Value - Price

**Reference Value**
> Price of what the customer views as the best substitute, <span style='color:#f7b731'>standard or reference point</span> that consumers use <span style='color:#f7b731'>evaluate the value of the product or service</span>.

**Differentiation Value**
> Value of differentiation attributes over the best substitute. It is <span style='color:#f7b731'>value that the customer perceived</span>.

What determines differentiation value is, availability, convenience, functionality, relationship and brand image.

Products released by companies is due to <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>hedonic adaptation</span>, which is the tendency of people to return to a relatively stable level of happiness.
### Marginal Utility

As customer continue to purchase a good or service, it will <span style='color:#f7b731'>continue to buy it if its marginal utility outweighs the value of the closest alternative</span>.

<span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Marginal utility</span>, is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>change in satisfaction</span> from an increase or decrease in consumption of one unit of a good and service.

As a <span style='color:#f7b731'>customer continues to buy the same type of good or service</span> will see its <span style='color:#f7b731'>marginal utility decrease</span>. Therefore companies are trying to <span style='color:#f7b731'>release the same type of product or service with different functionality</span> which will make it a different product all together.

# Developing A Pricing Strategy
---

Having access to <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>EVC</span> is a good way to start developing a pricing strategy. Which <span style='color:#f7b731'>leads to the maximum willingness to pay</span> (WTP). 

However customers varies in terms of their taste and EVC, thus there are other ways to develop a pricing strategy.

**Qualitative Methods**
> This includes interviews and observations (Early stages of the business)

However this method may have issues due to the difference between intention and behavior (Not accurate replies).

**Quantitative Methods**
> Using data to formulate a plan

This may have limitations in revealing hidden consumer needs.
## Using Big Data

Currently in this era, data is key and is everywhere. If companies can assess it and know how to use it, they will get an advantage over its competitors.

**Volume**
> The <span style='color:#f7b731'>large amount of data</span>, which makes conventional approaches and algorithms inefficient and less applicable

**Velocity**
> Real time or nearly real time information makes it possible for <span style='color:#f7b731'>companies</span> to be <span style='color:#f7b731'>more agile</span> than its competitors.

**Variety**
> Many sources where information can be retrieved and the format differs. These are called unstructured data (Messages, images, computer gathered data).

|                    Structured                    |                    Unstructured                     |
|:------------------------------------------------:|:---------------------------------------------------:|
|  Categorized and can be displayed in databases   |     Cannot be displayed in relational databases     |
|      Comprised of Numbers dates and String       |  Includes images, audio, videos, emails, files etc  |

While it is <span style='color:#f7b731'>easier to do analysis</span> on <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>structured data</span>, if companies have the means to <span style='color:#f7b731'>process and understand</span> <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>unstructured data</span>, then it will provide them with <span style='color:#f7b731'>in-depth understanding of consumer value and needs</span>.

## User Generated Content

It is as valuable as sources of customer value / needs for product development. Using machine learning, it improves the efficiency of identifying customer value / needs from UGC

# Price Discrimination
---
## First Degree

First degree pricing refers to <span style='color:#f7b731'>personalized pricing</span>. Every unit will be sold at a different price based on the customer.

It make sures that a it charges the maximum possible price for each customer willing to buy an product or service. This <span style='color:#f7b731'>captures all consumer surplus</span> as it attracts all possible consumers with different willingness.

How companies do this is through personal data such as geographic, infographic data and many more. 
## Second Degree

It involves charging consumers a <span style='color:#f7b731'>different price for the same amount or quality consumed</span>.

For example, quantity discount when buying in bulk or issues loyalty points to offer discounts on future products

Versioning is also considered second degree, where a company <span style='color:#f7b731'>charges people for different versions of a product</span>. Certain better features can be bought for a higher price as compared to standard versions. This is called <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>valued-subtracted versions</span>.

With the digital economy, it is easier to create different versions for digital goods and services.

### Bundling

It is a practice where <span style='color:#f7b731'>2 or more products are combined together and are being charged in one price</span>. For instance Microsoft office. It can be considered <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>second degree</span> discrimination, where its priced based on what is in the bundle

This also introduces new products and services.

## Third Degree

It involves charging a <span style='color:#f7b731'>different price to different customer groups</span>.

Companies will try and generate sales by segregating their customer base or market segments (Domestic and Industrial) with different price elasticities.

With IT it is easier to identify these segments and implement differential prices.

For example, a cinema charges a kids, student and adult tickets differently.

# Pricing Strategy In the Digital Economy
---
Companies must decide whether their strategic focus is to <mark class="hltr-orange">build up customer relationship and loyalty </mark>(this is achieved through a high customer benefit, the so called "value to the customer") or whether their focus is rather on a <mark class="hltr-orange">short term maximization of cash flows </mark>("value of the customer").
## Free Offering

<span style='color:#f7b731'>Certain products and services are provided to its consumers for free</span> such as the google search engine and WhatsApp. However these companies <span style='color:#f7b731'>make money in different ways</span> such as selling customer information or through others means like business advertising on their websites which both google and WhatsApp have.

WhatsApp used to charge its customers a subscription fee, however it is better to get a larger customer base than to charge a free on a smaller customer base and get smaller marginal profits.

**Advantages**
- It allows companies to have a larger user base and allow user-lock in
- It can offer a product free of charge in the market to create a new market (Launch Action) or to stimulate additional demand in off peak periods.

## Freemium Model

It is where the <span style='color:#f7b731'>basic product or service, is free to use</span>. But if a service with <span style='color:#f7b731'>higher quality is preferred, the user can opt for a subscription fee</span>.

One important <span style='color:#f7b731'>success</span> factor for the freemium concept is to <span style='color:#f7b731'>increase the conversion rate</span> (or reduce the correlation between users who pay nothing and users who pay the subscription fee).

It is not <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>second degree price</span> discrimination as the base model is free. 

For example google drive and OneDrive.

**Advantages**
- Obtain user base and attract customers since the base product is free
- It allows new ventures to scale up and attract a user base with out expanding resources on costly ad campaigns or a traditional sales force.

## Subscription Model

It is where customers <span style='color:#f7b731'>pay a certain fee per month / year</span> depending on the contract. It does not constitute a new form of pricing. Today even non-digital and traditional companies are using a subscription model through apps.

A subscription model can be used together with bundling where a consumer can pay a subscription to have access to a bundle of services or products. For example Netflix family bundle. 

**Advantages**
- generate customer loyalty by offering subscriptions
- Achieve a basic utilization of production and marketing capabilities
- Induce habit-based purchases (Healthy meal subscriptions)

## Dynamic Pricing

Depending on certain characteristics and time, the <span style='color:#f7b731'>prices might vary</span>. It is different from first degree price discrimination where <span style='color:#f7b731'>everyone will be offered the same prices</span>.

Come characteristics which might affect prices:
- Demand and Supply
- Competitor based pricing
	Prices will change <span style='color:#f7b731'>based on their competitors pricing</span>
- Distance Based pricing
	Prices varies depending on the <span style='color:#f7b731'>location of the consumer</span>
- Browsing based pricing
	Based on the <span style='color:#f7b731'>historical data</span>, the price will change based on the markets willingness to pay
- Past behavior pricing
	Based on <span style='color:#f7b731'>customer's transaction history</span> and thus their loyalty in the past determines the current price
- Devices based pricing
	The usage of <span style='color:#f7b731'>technology</span> which generates a query which <span style='color:#f7b731'>influences the price</span>.
