# Lock In
---
<span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Locking-in </span>incorporates all the mechanisms that a company uses to <span style='color:#f7b731'>keep its customers</span> by <span style='color:#f7b731'>establishing barriers to prevent customers from switching</span> to another supplier. This is also called <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>customer retention</span>.

**Churning**
>Is defined as the act of a customer switching to another service or product offered by competitors 

The <span style='color:#f7b731'>lower the churning rate</span> the more successful the company is.

The reason to keep existing customer is because, <span style='color:#f7b731'>acquiring customers is expansive</span>. Thus companies wants to exploit various lock in mechanisms to reduce churning.

With a high lock in rate, the more power a company has and this will result in anti competition, anti consumer and anti security (Forming of monopolies).
## What is Lock-In to Market Players

**Customers**

Customers will want to <span style='color:#f7b731'>avoid lock-in to maximize future utility</span> as bring locking in will reduce future options for switching.

Customers will be locked in if the <span style='color:#f7b731'>cost of switching is higher than the gains from switching</span>.

**Local Firms**

Firms wants to lock in customer as this will <span style='color:#f7b731'>grow their customer base</span> and in turn they will <span style='color:#f7b731'>gain more market power</span>.

Captivate customer who are reluctant to substitute because of high costs are a good asset to them. And to lock in customers companies will use diverse strategies.

**Government**

Lock-in strategies leads to <span style='color:#f7b731'>monopolies which can result in market failure and reduced customer benefits</span>. Thus governments review lock in strategies.

Governments regulate the market with regulation put in place to ensure a healthy market.

# Switching Cost
---
It is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>direct and indirect cost for customers</span> to switch to a new supplier of a good or service (Customer switching cost).

The cost of preventing a switch depends on the firms competitive advantage.

**Total Switching Cost** = Cost to stop customer from switching + Price of product or service

For companies to <span style='color:#f7b731'>attract customers to switch is to lower their own switching cost</span>, either by increasing their own product value or decrease their competitors value or decrease their prices.

# Lock in Classification
---
## Contractual Commitments

The intention of <span style='color:#f7b731'>switching will go up at the end of the contract</span>. Thus being innovative can extend a customers contract as they are no longer locked in their contractual obligation.

For example, Singtel who offers a subscription package which not only allows customers to buy phones at a cheaper rate but it also includes other things like WIFI and television channels which makes it harder to customer to switch.

Bundling pricing strategy can lock in customers due to contractual commitments on the customer side.

## Brand Specific Training

Some companies will produce similar products that <span style='color:#f7b731'>function similarly but operate very differently</span> from its competitors. Due to the nature of technology, the physiological comfort learning cost becomes powerful to lock in customers.

For example if a customer were to spend time to learn how to use apples' OS then to him it will be difficult to switch to an android phone as firstly, they spend time to learn how to use it and secondly the customer will need to spend more time to learn to use an android phone again.

## Information & Databases

Data conversion costs and costs of losing data is things customers and companies are sensitive about.

For businesses and individuals, data must be stored in a particular physical form and format. This may result in loss of information or instigate major work to be done to convert the software.

Even if this is not the case, the belief that information may be lost for some unspecified reason may be enough to cause lock in. Losing data due to <span style='color:#f7b731'>conversion is in terms of the benefits you get based on the personal data companies have</span>. For example Netflix, if you were to switch then the recommendations will be lost and you need time to get back those recommendations.

Database systems tend to grow because of the fear that the removal of an old database may cause loss of information, or that deleted information may turn out to be useful after it has been removed. It can also cause down time, loss in productivity and potential legal liabilities.

## Search Cost

This has to do with the <span style='color:#f7b731'>cost and time it takes for customers to find a particular product and service</span> among millions of other products. If companies can make it easier to search for products it can cause a lock-in for customers.

This is mostly for companies such as Amazon, Shopee, Airbnb, video platforms and more.

## Loyalty Programs

It is a way to <span style='color:#f7b731'>stimulate customers to purchase their products and services rather than those of competitors</span> as they earn rewards from the company.

This is similar to club membership where members have access to goods and services not available to non members or they gain an advantage.

To make a successful loyalty program, the <span style='color:#f7b731'>structure has to be simple and direct</span>, the faster and easier a customer can learn the loyalty program the faster they will sign up.

With digital technology, it can efficiently enable loyalty programs by leveraging real-time tracing and digital incentive features
- Personalized Rewards
- Gamification Features
- Customer Relationship management Systems

Results suggest that badging and leader boarding promote sales by 21.5% and 22.5% in the treatment period, respectively, whereas couponing delivers a more potent effect of 31.7%.

## Ecosystem Integration

Ecosystem integration refers to the <span style='color:#f7b731'>seamless interconnection of various software applications, platforms, tools, technologies , and services</span>, creating a unified and cohesive digital environment

Tesla's integration of vehicles, solar products, and energy storage solutions like the Powerwall and Powerpack creates an ecosystem that encourages customers to adopt multiple Tesla products

Google's ecosystem integration: once a user is accustomed to Google's interface, services, and the seamless integration between them, it becomes more challenging and less appealing to switch to another service or platform.

# Lock-In & Market Failure
---

Locking in results in a winner-takes-all situation as as those with lower churning rates have a larger market power. This increases the chances of a <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>monopoly</span> which reduces innovation and keeping high prices.

When consumers are locked into a particular vendor or product, it becomes <span style='color:#f7b731'>challenging for new entrants or competitors to gain a foothold in the market</span>.

In the absence of significant competition and with a locked in consumer base, firms might have more power to set prices higher than they would in a more competitive market and might lack the incentive to innovate or improve their products or adopt better alternatives.

In markets with strong lock in effects, vendors might not be incentivized to be transparent about all product features or potential future costs, knowing that once consumers are locked in, it is hard for them to switch. This can lead to situations where consumers make decisions without full information.

Even if these competitors offer superior or more cost effective products, the costs and inconveniences associated with switching may deter consumers from making a change.

Brand image also helps in making a product more attractable to customer. Take for instance apple, it is so prominent that new customer will gravitate towards it.

## Combat Lock-In Failure

Technology standard is caused by lock ins. Lock in within the free market leads to the potential of making an inferior choice

Governments are there to ensure that the market is regulated and ensure safety for all parties by setting rules and regulations companies need to follow such that these companies don't get too carried away.

One of the most important aspects of <span style='color:#f7b731'>market regulation is to reduce the lock in capabilities of service providers to enhance competition</span>.

For example, contractual lock in and some loyalty programs may be regulated by law
