# Economic Impact of AI
---
How much of an affect does AI have on the economy will determine the future adoption of AI and its usage. This <span style='color:#f7b731'>impact is assessed at a individual, business and societal levels</span>
## Individual assessments

1) **Productivity**

In general AI has a <span style='color:#20bf6b'>positive impact on productivity</span>, <span style='color:#f7b731'>enabling automation of tasks</span>, affecting <span style='color:#f7b731'>capital intensive</span> sectors like manufacturing and transport

<span style='color:#eb3b5a'>Slower productivity due to AI</span> can be attributed to;
- Low investment levels
- Limited talent pool
- Stringent regulations
- Cultural attitudes
- No educational focus on AI and technology

2) **Employment**

The introduction of AI has <span style='color:#20bf6b'>increased the number of jobs</span> as with AI it <span style='color:#f7b731'>increases revenue and employment</span> in firms because it has the potential to create new products and thus requires people to support AI related activities

This allows people to <span style='color:#f7b731'>focus on higher-value and higher-touch tasks</span> that often require interpersonal interactions.

However not all jobs are safe as <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>job polarization</span> is probable, where lower-paid jobs which are manual and cognitive are at a high risk of being replaced by AI and automation

Possible ways to prevent job polarization
- Reskilling and upskilling
- Work alongside AI
- Good in critical thinking and problem solving
- People management
- Creativity
- Emotional intelligence

## Business assessments

1) **Patents / Mergers & Acquisitions**

<span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Patents</span> is used to recognise and rewards inventors for their commercially-successful invention, which has been on the rise

The boom in AI-related patents is that they not only <span style='color:#f7b731'>disclose AI techniques and applications </span>but often <span style='color:#f7b731'>refer to a field or industry of application</span> (Vehicles, Areospace). Companies like IBM, Samsung and MS are the top patent holders

<span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Mergers & Acquisitions</span> (M&A), it is the process of buying a company and merging it together. Big companies have been <span style='color:#f7b731'>buying up a lot of start-ups focusing on AI related things</span>, but has been declining due to covid

2) **Size of AI investments by business**

There has been a <span style='color:#f7b731'>continuous increase of investments</span> in AI over the years. In 2021, the global corporate investment reached 94 billion US dollars
## Societal / Economical assessments

1) **GDP (Gross Domestic Product)**

AI has <span style='color:#20bf6b'>increased the GDP</span> of many countries around the world. This is because with an increased consumption of AI, there will be <span style='color:#f7b731'>more data touch points, better insights, better products</span> and therefore, more consumption

Example, using AI to search for poverty

## AI Management

<span style='color:#2d98da'>Taylorism</span>, is the scientific management aimed to <span style='color:#f7b731'>improve economic and labor efficiently</span> by analyzing and streaming workflows

Scientific management requires a <span style='color:#f7b731'>high level of managerial control over employee work practices</span> and entails a higher ratio of managerial workers to laborers than previous management methods

The newest version of <span style='color:#2d98da'>Taylorism</span> currently does the same thing managing workload but <span style='color:#f7b731'>using AI instead</span>
	 Determining the location and movement considering the delivery order (Follow the instructions of AI). This <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>decreases the value of skilled labour and deprivation</span> of job <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>autonomy</span>

**Autonomy**
> The joy of <span style='color:#f7b731'>taking responsibility</span>, exerting influence in the work organization and<span style='color:#f7b731'> coping without supervisory control</span>

**Meaningful work**
> It can be classified under fundamental human need to satisfy their inherent interest in freedom, autonomy and dignity

Example : Cleaners who streamline and organize their work, accumulate knowledge, feel confident in their skills and care about customer satisfaction

The profit-driven platforms are very strict in sharing data with riders/drivers and third-party organizations due to <span style='color:#f7b731'>security issues and criticisms</span>, such as uber and their AI dispatch system

Uber uses psychological manipulation, to make individual have no control of their own status, this is called <span style='color:#2d98da'>irresistible stimuli</span> or the <span style='color:#2d98da'>ludic loop</span>

However, <span style='color:#f7b731'>algorithmic control increases individual utility</span> rather than platforms utility

**Give choice to workers**
> It is a collaborative <span style='color:#f7b731'>relationship between workers and algorithms</span>. Involving workers in the decision-making processes leads to <span style='color:#20bf6b'>higher levels of perceived autonomy and competence </span>among workers. Not only is this beneficial for the workers, but it can also benefit gig platforms by <span style='color:#20bf6b'>preventing worker turnover</span>

It also <span style='color:#20bf6b'>benefits the health</span> of the workers

## AI Crime

**Deepfakes**
> A technology built using <span style='color:#f7b731'>generative adversarial network</span> (GAN) which can <span style='color:#f7b731'>create fake videos or audio</span> that mimic the appearance or voice of a person. It utilises both deep learning and AI

This <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>deepfakes</span> can lead to <span style='color:#f7b731'>sexualised crime</span>, which is a tool for identity theft, extortion, sexual exploitation, reputational damage, ridicule, intimidation and harassment 

AI can also create <span style='color:#f7b731'>fake text-based content</span> or fake reviews, this caused misleading realistic looking reviews which can be difficult to detect

## AI Authoritarianism

It is the usage of AI to generate interventions or map out individuals for <span style='color:#f7b731'>political purposes</span>

Political campaigns have used AI algorithms to analyze social media behavior and <span style='color:#f7b731'>target individuals with personalized political advertisements</span> which can influence voters.

Examples: 
- Post information on Twitter and Instagram to support specific political perspective
- AI write a post or make a meme.

Some law enforcement agencies use AI to predict where crimes may occur or who might commit them. While not always political in nature, such systems can lead to controversial interventions if they reflect or reinforce existing biases

# Ethical AI Design
---
## Government Regulation

Governments can <span style='color:#f7b731'>establish guidelines</span> and clarify the attribution of responsibility. The AI act or GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation) states that high risk AI system, the government will intervene 

The norm is that <span style='color:#f7b731'>individuals or entities</span> who use the technology will <span style='color:#f7b731'>bare the responsibility</span>. Also companies commercializing technologies should discuss with the government before launching their services. 

There might be a need for a <span style='color:#f7b731'>screening and oversight function</span> to prevent the use of AI for inappropriate purposes

The use of prohibited systems and the violation of the data-governance provisions when using high-risk systems will incur the <span style='color:#f7b731'>largest potential fines</span>

As currently proposed, high-risk systems would be subject to the <span style='color:#f7b731'>largest set of requirements</span>, including human oversight, transparency, cybersecurity, risk management, data quality, monitoring, and reporting obligations

## Ethical AI Design

### Privacy Protection

It is to operate AI while <span style='color:#f7b731'>complying with data protection laws</span> such as GDPR

The government requires companies to implement robust data protection measures, including 
- anonymization
- data encryption
- obtaining informed consent from individuals whose data is being used
- ensuring transparency in data processing practices

### AI Transparency and Explainability

It is the efforts to make AI systems' operations and decision-making processes <span style='color:#f7b731'>understandable</span>. This means AI firms make decision-making processes <span style='color:#f7b731'>fair and comprehensible</span>

Firms should <span style='color:#f7b731'>adopt interpretable</span> AI Models, especially for critical decision-making processes

Firms <span style='color:#f7b731'>document</span> how AI systems make decisions and <span style='color:#f7b731'>share information with the local consumers</span>. This documentation aids in external audits and monitoring and allows for the <span style='color:#f7b731'>explanation of decision-making processes</span> for the local consumer (e.g., driver in Uber case, customer in e-commerce platform)

Using explainable AI models in financial institutions to <span style='color:#f7b731'>ensure transparent</span> loan approval decisions. Additionally, regulatory bodies like the U.S. Federal Trade Commission (FTC) have begun to scrutinize AI systems for transparency and fairness in various industries.

### Algorithmic Bias and Fairness

It is to address how AI algorithms and their decision-making can be <span style='color:#f7b731'>biased</span>, organizations are <span style='color:#f7b731'>taking steps to detect and mitigate such biases</span>

Some companies like Amazon has biased AI system, which has been adjusted to ensure fairness in employment decisions;
- Techniques like <span style='color:#f7b731'>re-weighting, re-ranking, or adversarial training</span> can be used to minimize bias in model predictions
- In some cases, <span style='color:#f7b731'>sensitive attributes like race can be removed</span> from the input data to prevent the model from learning biased patterns based on these attributes
- If there is an <span style='color:#f7b731'>underrepresentation</span> of certain groups in the data, <span style='color:#f7b731'>collect more data from those groups</span> or <span style='color:#f7b731'>employ techniques like oversampling or importance sampling</span> to balance the dataset

Regulatory bodies and guidelines, such as the <span style='color:#f7b731'>AI Ethics Guidelines from the European Commission</span>, are emphasizing the importance of fairness assessments and bias mitigation in AI applications across various domains
