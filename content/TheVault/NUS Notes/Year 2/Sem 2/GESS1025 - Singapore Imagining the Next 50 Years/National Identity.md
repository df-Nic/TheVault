# What is National Identity
---
A **national identity** is just <span style='color:var(--mk-color-orange)'>1 type of social identity</span>.
>*Social identity* is a perceived identity among various groups (*Male*, *Female*, *Student*)

The **strength of a national identity** is a <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>conceptual measurement</span>. There can be many ways such as:
- The will to defend the country
- National values
- Food
- Languages

> [!success] National Identity in a Positive Sense
> When describing national identity, it can be viewed in a positive light by <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>showing the positives</span> such as:
>- Staying to defend the country
>- Friendliness to others
>- Multicultural

> [!missing] National Identity in a Negative Sense
> However it can also be view negatively, by <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>displaying unwanted intentions</span> to others such as:
>- Distaste to certain groups of people
>- Treating others not from their own country unequally
>>This causes an <span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>ingroup identity</span>

# Meritocracy

The term meritocracy means, <span style='color:var(--mk-color-green)'>based on merit</span> (*Competencies*) and <span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>not based on inheritance</span>. This is also known as <span style='color:var(--mk-color-turquoise)'>performance-based</span> or <span style='color:var(--mk-color-turquoise)'>competence-based</span> meritocracy.

> [!info] Fairness

> 
> Singapores does display fiarness by helping the needy financally, allowing them to support and give them the same opportunities as everyone.
## Racial Integration

There is <span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>no existing model for interracial living</span>, but Singapore has developed it own model for interracial living.

> [!abstract] Group Representation Constituency
>A constitution by Singapore which states that <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>no political party can play on race</span>. For instance a full malay party cannot just use all Malay candidates and play on issues faced by the Malay's. 
> 

One important factor to ensuring racial integration is through <b><mark style='background:var(--mk-color-green)'>not taking advantages of certain races</mark></b>. The <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>leaders cannot use race or religion for political gains</span>.

> [!abstract] Housing Policies
> In Singapore, there is a integration policies which has a <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>quota of how many said races can stay at a particular HDB</span>.
> 
> This <span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>does volate the freedome of people or human rights</span> for some. But by nature <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>people tend to congregate with people they are comfortable with</span>.
> 
> > This is also the **same for school policies**, to prevent segregation of races due to school allocations.

> [!danger] What can Affect Multiracialism
> Racial issues **exist everywhere** around the world. And these <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>issues can also indirectly impact the difference races in Singapore</span>
> 
> For example, if the Chinese were ill treated by some other race in some other country then the Chinese in Singapore might have a negative view on people of the same race in Singapore.

<b><mark style='background:var(--mk-color-green)'>Equality amongst races</mark></b> is another important factor, like [[National Identity#Meritocracy|meritocracy]], <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>regardless of race</span> **everyone should be given equal opportunity**.
