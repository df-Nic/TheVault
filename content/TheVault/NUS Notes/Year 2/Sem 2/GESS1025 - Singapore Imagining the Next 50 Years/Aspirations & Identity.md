# Singapore as a Utopia
---

- The Garden of Eden state

> [!quote] A perfect city was imagined as an island city by Thomas Mors

How does <span style='color:var(--mk-color-orange)'>Singapore fit into this Utopia</span> world:

# Charting Futures
---
The term "The world is your oyster" suggests unlimited possibilities but this requires some **planning ahead**.

This is the case so Singapore's policies to keep herself relevant.
## Entrepreneurship

Singapore strategy for **building a strong economy** does not only rely on multinational companies to setup here but also the <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>local businesses to flourish & prosper</span></b> (*the government entity Sprint aims to do just that*).
### Roles of MNCs

The **economic development board was set up as a primary agency to bring in MNCs**. This is to create more jobs for its citizens.

However <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>doing business in Singapore is expensive</span></b>. Which might make it hard for MNCs to make a profit and thus might not stay in Singapore for an extended period of time (*Losing jobs*).

This can cause a <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>disruption to the economy</span></b> if Singapore heavily relies on MNCs.
### Pipeline of Entrepreneurs

Singapore needs **young entrepreneurs** however it is <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>not easy</span></b>:
- Some lose everything
- Some even after a few years are still living salary to salary

Through Singapore wants more entrepreneurs, if they cannot handle the stress then it is better to be an employee, if not it can <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>disrupt the economy</span></b> because people enter and too many exit (*losing everything and relying on the government*).
## Education

One of Singapore's policies was to have a <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>liberal arts education</span></b> (*undergraduates take scientific & humanities subjects*).

The brought about 2 new universities
- **Yale NUS college**
- **Singapore university of technology and design**

The reason for this is that the <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>job market is volatile</span></b>. It is possible that <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>jobs or entire industries will disappear</span></b>, leaving what you studied to be irrelevant.

> [!example] Uber which is drastically changing the taxi industry

The goal is to prepare people to have <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-green)'>diverse skill sets and applying it in real world context</span></b>.
## Heritage

Buildings are about **30 years old** will be conserved. As the long time frame will allow us to have a <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>objective view of the past</span></b> (*1 generation*).

Conservation is both an **art** and a **science**.

There are <span style='color:var(--mk-color-orange)'>2 levels of protection</span>:
1) **Monument status** by the preservation of sites and monuments under the ministry of culture
2) **Conservation** under the URA which reports to the ministry of national development

> [!info] Monuments are based on historical significant 

> [!info] Conservation it is based the important of its architecture and urbanism
> - Is it beautiful?
> - is it unique?
> - Contributions to the streets or urban scape?
> - Communities?
> - Identity of an area?
