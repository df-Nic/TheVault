# Social Inequality in Singapore
---
Singapore is a <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>meteoric society</span></b>. <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-turquoise)'>Mediocracy</span></b> is a system for sorting, selecting, and then differentially rewarding people; it is a system for legitimizing the process and outcomes of sorting, based on narrow notions of what is worth rewarding and what is not.

It basically <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>rewards people based on the hard work they put in</span></b>.

**Singapore's narrative of the nation**, is its <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-green)'>success in developing into a shining global city within a few decades</span></b> (*Its successful development*).

> [!info] Mobility
> The movement—usually of individuals but sometimes of whole groups—between different positions within the system of social stratification in any society.
> 
> It does not mean moving from one place to another it can be **movement from a lower class to a higher class**.

National leaders **institutionalize meritocracy** through <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>formal education to promise opportunities for moving upward</span></b>. Internalization of the nation’s narratives: “You get rewarded as long as you work hard.”

Differential rewards based on the results of evaluating individuals’ academic performance like examinations.

> [!info] Competition
> Intends to make it fair for everyone but positioning individuals unequally based on various criteria

When talking about meritocracy we need to know the criteria used for selection & evaluation, it is <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>neither neutral nor universal</span></b> (*normal to someone might not be the same as others*).

> [!question] What values do the national narratives reward?
> **Rewardable**: values that can be <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>translated into assets</span></b> to contribute to individuals and families’ upward movement in both material and symbolic ways.
> 
> **Not rewardable**: values that are <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>not legible</span></b> (*especially when they are associated with the lower-income*).

**Misrecognition and the stories about failures**: those who cannot get their qualities recognized as merit deserves to be where they are (*you did not go university because you did not work hard, which might not be the case*).
