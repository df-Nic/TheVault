# Goal
---
>[!tldr] Address how Indian organisations/initiatives in Singapore work to address an issue faced by Indians in Singapore.
# What Must be Done
---
- Identify the issue (*Can be a problem, desire, wants, needs*)
- Underlying causes or amplifiers of the issue
- Identify organisations/initiatives that directly address the issue
- Explain how this organisation/initiative addressed the identified issue
- Identity blind spots/gaps and offer suggestions

>[!important] Must have at least 3 engagements with members of the Indian community
>
>Some examples are, interviews, survey events
