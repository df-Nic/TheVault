# Perspectives of Landscapes
---
Showing a **same landscape** to different people might <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>not necessary mean everyone will share the same perspective as you</span>.

> [!seealso]  Perspective of landscapes
> When viewing a landscape, it does not only show what is visible but it can also <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>show something behind the scenes</span> (*Why is the landscape as such, or hisorical significance*).
> 
> And this is why people have these different prespectives.
## 10 Ways of Viewing Landscapes

1) **History**
> Its not really about historical significance but rather <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>what can we learn about the past from this image</span>.

2) **Place**
> Its more about the landscape and <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>why is it so significant</span> to the person or <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>why it is different from other places</span>.

3) **Artifact**
> It is about how can we <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>learn about the process</span> (*What people did*) based on what is in view.

4) **Wealth**
> Its not only about your view on a locations financial status, but rather <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>how would an appraiser place a value</span> on the landscape (*Amenities , anything special around the place*)

5) **Nature**
> It not about trees, or greenery but the <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>relationship between human and nature</span>. In **relation to nature humans are miniscule and subordinate**. It can also be nature in its pristine condition.

6) **Habitat**
> Its different from nature as it focuses more on the <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>continuous process of building a relationship with nature</span> (*Thus more on the process*).

7) **System**
> It describes how this <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>landscape plays a part in a particular system</span>. For instance a river can play apart in the hydrological circuit.

8) **Problem**
> Not really how this landscape solves some problems but rather <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>evidence of a existing problem that needs to be fixed</span>.

9) **Ideology**
> It **focuses more on what can be seen** and its <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>underlying ideas</span>. Its the view we see as a symbol of values, governing ideas, and underlying philosophies of a culture.

10) **Aesthetic**
> Focuses more on the <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>artistic quality of the location</span>, like the different colors, patters, placements and how it aesthetically appeal to others.
