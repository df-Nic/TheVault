# Course Requirements
---
## Community Engagement Project

This semester's engagement project is a creative one. Meaning there is no restrictions to it and it will be based on the creative ideas of the students.

Topics can be based on your interest and how it can link with the community, for example:
- An interactive map of meal delivery/food donation drives by Indian groups and organisations
- A poster on how artistes’ unions have ensured continued state patronage of Indian art forms
- A short video on how cricket leagues unite various Indian communities
