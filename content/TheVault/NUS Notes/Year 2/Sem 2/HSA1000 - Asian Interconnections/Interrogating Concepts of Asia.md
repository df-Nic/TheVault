## Western Imperialism

The Asia we know today, came about from <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>western imperialism</span>. Some countries were colonised by Europeans and after the second world war, many of these places gain independence.

However even **after they gain independence**, <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>certain stereotypes persisted</span> till this day.
