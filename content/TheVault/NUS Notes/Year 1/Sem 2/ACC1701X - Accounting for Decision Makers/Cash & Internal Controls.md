# Bank Reconciliations
---
Usually the bank will mail a monthly <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>bank statement</span> to <span style='color:#f7b731'>indicate the ending cash balance</span> for the company, however usually, the <span style='color:#f7b731'>companies internal records</span> for the <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>ending cash balance are different</span>.

As a <span style='color:#f7b731'>internal cash control procedure</span>, the company will do a <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>reconciliation</span> to reconcile the differences.

A <span style='color:#fa8231'>bank statement will have</span> :
- Cash balance at the **beginning** of the month
- Deposits
- Withdrawals
- Additions
- Checks processed
- The bank's calculated balance at the end for the company

## Performing Reconciliations

![[Performing Bank Reconiliations.png|center|600]]

**Deposits in transit**
> Deposits that are not processed by the bank

**Outstanding Checks**
>Checks that are processed from the company cash account by the bank but has not deducted by the bank.

**Bank Errors**
> Numerical or other types of errors made by the bank

On the **company side**, for these <span style='color:#fa8231'>3 above</span> there is <span style='color:#f7b731'>no need to make adjusting entries</span> as it is already recorded.

**NSF (Not Sufficient Funds)**
> Checks that have <span style='color:#f7b731'>bounced</span>, due to insufficient funds in the payer's bank account

**Adjusting Entry :**
- <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit Account Receivable</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b> since the cash is not in the companies account.

**Miscellaneous Items Not Recorded**
> Other items like (a) service fees <span style='color:#f7b731'>charged by the bank</span> or (b) <span style='color:#f7b731'>interest from the bank</span> must be adjusted as well

**Adjusting Entry :**
- For (a) - <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit Service Expense</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b>
- For (b) - <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit Cash</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit interest revenue</mark></b>

**Bank debits / transfers**
>They are <span style='color:#f7b731'>deductions made by the bank</span> and have not been recorded

**Adjusting Entry :**
- <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit Expense</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b>

**Bank Credits / Direct Deposits**
>These are <span style='color:#f7b731'>deposits into the companies account</span> before being recorded into the books

**Adjusting Entry :**
- <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit Cash</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit accounts receivable</mark></b>

**Book Errors**
> Numerical or other types of errors made by the bank

# Petty Cash
---
For<span style='color:#fa8231'> small payments</span>, companies will set up a <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>petty cash fund</span>, to <span style='color:#20bf6b'>save time and effort</span>.

To <span style='color:#fa8231'>establish the fund</span>, just to the following entry :
- <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit Petty Cash</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b>

Any <span style='color:#fa8231'>payments from the fund</span> are <span style='color:#f7b731'>documented with petty cash vouchers</span> and the established fund should be balance plus sum of vouchers.

To <span style='color:#fa8231'>replenish the fund</span> just to the following entry :
- <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit All the expenses</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b>

What if there is a <span style='color:#fa8231'>human error in the entry</span>, on replenish:
- <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>Debit All the expenses</mark></b> and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b>
- In addition, <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>debit Cash Short and Over</mark></b> account and <b><mark style='background:#f7b731'>credit cash</mark></b> which is the <span style='color:#f7b731'>amount that it differs by</span>

This <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>cash short and over</span> account, the <span style='color:#f7b731'>cash shortage is a debit, cash over is a credit</span> (Overcount / Undercount).

# Internal Controls
---
<span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Internal controls</span> <span style='color:#20bf6b'>ensures integrity</span> of financial introduction, which includes :
1) **Control environment**
2) **Control activities**
## Control Environment

**Management philosophy and operating style**
> <span style='color:#f7b731'>Actions, policies and procedures</span> that reflect the overall attitudes of <span style='color:#f7b731'>top management about control</span> and its importance to the company

**Organisation Structure**
> <span style='color:#f7b731'>Identify</span> clear lines of <span style='color:#f7b731'>authority</span> and <span style='color:#f7b731'>responsibility</span>

**Audit committee**
> <span style='color:#f7b731'>Independent board of directors, audit committee, external and internal auditors</span> who exercise independent oversight of management (Ideally, the chairperson of the board and the CEO should be different and not related)

Overall leadership is important to sustain a strong control environment, the board, audit committee and senior management <span style='color:#f7b731'>establish the tone at the top</span> regarding the importance of internal control.

With a <span style='color:#fa8231'>strong control environment</span>, <span style='color:#f7b731'>everyone responds conscientiously</span> to the control policies and procedures.

## Control Actions

### Preventative Controls

**Segregation of duties**
><span style='color:#f7b731'>Not one employee should be doing all the work</span>, certain things should be done by different department and by different people

Things like authorization, record keeping and asset custody for transactions should be done by different people.

**Proper procedures for authorization**
>Every transaction must have a <span style='color:#f7b731'>proper authorization</span>. The approval matrix should be documented and compiled with through internal controls

**Physical control over assets and records**
><span style='color:#f7b731'>Physical safeguards</span> to protect assets and records, such as cameras, vaults, security and alarms
### Detective Controls

**Adequate documents and record**
><span style='color:#f7b731'>Transaction records should be kept safely</span> as they are evidence which is used to review any transaction for appropriate authorization

**Independent checks on performance**
><span style='color:#f7b731'>Annual external audits</span> or regular internal audits, <span style='color:#f7b731'>mandatory leave</span> for sensitive positions, periodic <span style='color:#f7b731'>staff rotations</span> or transfers which can be <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>costly</span> or <span style='color:#f7b731'>independent verifications and reconciliations</span>.

## Cash Controls

Using the list of <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>control actions</span>, here is how to ensure <span style='color:#fa8231'>strong cash controls</span>

**Segregating duties for handling and recording cash**
>Without it <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>people can steal cash</span> and not record the transactions in the accounting records

**Deposit cash receipts daily**
><span style='color:#f7b731'>Prevent accumulation</span> of large amounts <span style='color:#f7b731'>of cash</span> on hand

**Large payments are made with prenumbered checks**
>Payments by cash, conceal fraud and irregularities (Bribery), while payment by checks are well documented

**Prepare monthly bank reconciliation to account for cash**

# Fraud
---
<span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Fraud</span> is the intention to <span style='color:#f7b731'>manipulate</span> financial results, where there are <span style='color:#fa8231'>3 types</span> :
1) **Asset misappropriation**
2) **Corruption** (Bribes and kickbacks)
3) **Financial misstatement or omissions** (Faking the values)

**Unintentional Errors**
> <span style='color:#f7b731'>Wrongly record transactions</span>, be it the account or the value

**Earning Management**
>It is a intentional manipulation of financial results <b><mark style='background:#20bf6b'>legally</mark></b>

To meet internal targets, external expectations, smooth income and window dress for public stock offering or loan application.

**2 common ways are:**
1) Modify operating or investing decisions. E.g., changing the timing to invest or purchase goods and delaying expenditure
2) Modify accounting estimates and methods. E.g., changing accounting estimate of loss allowance for accounts receivable.
