## Why is there Migration in Singapore

People migrate because they have been deported from their country or they want to seek <span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>economic and political opportunities</span>.

**Migration** can be defined as the <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>movement of individuals from one territory to another</span></b>. And there can be many reasons for this movement:
- **Economic**
- **Political**
- **Social**
- **Cultural**

It can also be due to <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>push and full factors</span></b>:
- **Push factors**: Something that forces individuals to moves
- **Pull factors**: Something that makes moving attractive

There are <span style='color:var(--mk-color-orange)'>2 categories of migration</span>:
- **Vagabonds**: Financial poor migrants who move because <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>they have to move</span></b> (*war, economic, political*). Basically people who <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>involuntary</span></b> move
- **Tourist**: People who <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>voluntary</span></b> move

How does <span style='color:var(--mk-color-orange)'>countries view migration</span>:
- **Classical** model, encouraging economical migrants who are looking for jobs
- **Colonial** model, past colonial countries who receive migrants mostly from their ex-colonies
- **Guest-worker** model, migrants are welcomed temporary but have no rights to citizenship
- **Illegal** model, come and stay in the country illegally
## Race & Ethnicity

The <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>majority group</span></b> who host these immigrants are known as <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-turquoise)'>host society</span></b>. Many people have come up with different theories of how migrants can fit into society in terms of **assimilation** and **integration**.
### Assimilation

> [!info] Assimilation
> Defined the process in which the <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>characteristics of a minorly migrant group which are subsumed, absorbed or adapted into the characteristics of the host society</span></b>.
#### Assimilation Models

##### Classical Model

It proposes that the assimilation of migrant groups into the host society is linear in that such <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>convergence is neat and eventual</span></b>.

As they interact with the host society they absorb more of the values norms and culture. This is especially true for the migrant's children who see the host society as the home they were born.

**Stages of assimilation in the classical model**
1) Pick up local language and cultural practices
2) Enjoy closer social relations with the host society
3) Marriages with the host society and the migrants
4) Merging of identities (*hybrid culture like the peranakans*)
5) End to prejudice and discrimination
##### Racial or Ethnic Disadvantage Model

Due to the cultural differences, migrants are often <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>unable to assimilate fully with their host society</span></b>.

There might be **some advantages** to having this differences but it still causes <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>racial and ethnic discrimination</span></b>.

> [!question] Why can they not assimilate
> - Certain laws prevent them
> - Different in social economic status
##### Segmented Assimilation Model

<b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>Certain economic barriers</span></b> (*access to education jobs or other opportunities*) can migrants who are at a disadvantage <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>harder to assimilate</span></b>.

These people might reject assimilation and "close ranks". Creating risks between the 2 groups. **All this depends on the migrant** and how the country perceives these migrants.
## Social Media

How does Singapore view race in the past, between people who have <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>different skin colour, there was a distinct biological difference</span></b>.

Some will perceived that having different skin colour means differences in intelligence, behaviour, culture and other non-biological characteristics, there are known as <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-turquoise)'>stereotypes</span></b> (*social constructs*). It can also <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>marginalise and exalt certain people</span></b>.

Thus we should <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>not understand race from a biological or fixed standpoint</span></b>. **Race is a contemporary society is decidedly visual**, because it varies from people to people on why they judge these groups.

**Social media** has made the <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>experience of different races</span></b> and ethnic groups <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>based on what we see online</span></b>.

Many use <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>clickbait or false information to get more shares</span></b> and thus more widespread coverage.
