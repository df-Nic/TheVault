# Human Intelligence VS AI
---

**Intelligence**
> Defined as the comprehensive capabilities of an individual to <span style='color:#f7b731'>reason about a problem and solve it through cognitive and learning abilities</span>

**Artificial Intelligence (AI)**
> It is a <span style='color:#f7b731'>simulation</span> of human intelligence processed by machines or computer systems

The goal of AI is to <span style='color:#f7b731'>assist humans</span>, making a smarter, better living society

What is a **perfect AI**;
- Human-like
- Can solve mathematical problems (Problems, Theorems)
- Common-sense reasoning (Illegal and Ethical)
- Expert Knowledge (Deep understanding based on profession like law)
	 If it can have experts knowledge it can be <span style='color:#f7b731'>viable for commercial use</span>
- Social Behaviour
- Rational-like
- Achieve goals and have a performance measure

## Defining AI

**Acting Humanly**
> Ability to perform tasks indistinguishable from a human. This emphasizes on <span style='color:#f7b731'>mimicking human behaviour
</span>

There is a test called the <span style='color:#2d98da'>turning test</span> which determines if a <span style='color:#f7b731'>program qualifies as an AI through human interrogation</span>
1) An interrogator will have to determine which of the 2 entitites is the computer
2) If the interrogator fails to identify then the computer passes the test

As of now <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>no AI has fully passed the test</span>

**Thinking Humanly**
> Machines to <span style='color:#f7b731'>mimic human though process</span>. And to achieve this, knowledge about human cognition is used to develop models that simulate human thought process

**Thinking Rationally**
> The logical aspect of <span style='color:#f7b731'>problem solving and decision making</span> (Logic & Reasoning). This emphasizes logical and optimal decision making

Example: If all computers use energy, and energy generates heat, the the AI should know that computers generate heat

## Views On AI

**Weak / Narrow AI**
> It only <mark class="hltr-orange">specialises in performing one task</mark> and no more, it does not engage in conversations, learning or information gathering

Examples: AI-chatbots to schedule appointments or set reminders (Popular in the medical field)

**Strong AI**
> It can <mark class="hltr-orange">perform a variety of functions</mark>. It teaches itself to solve new problems and have emotions, a sense of purpose and even humor

Examples: AI gives treatment, does surgery, learn diverse symptoms independently (AI stitching on a grape)

Not always <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Strong AI</span> is better, companies a leveraging on both for their businesses

## AI Vs Statistics

### AI

AI models are designed to make the most <mark class="hltr-orange">accurate predictions possible</mark>

AI is technically true; it <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>does not give a particularly explicit or satisfying answer</span>. It also deals with<span style='color:#f7b731'> building systems that can learn from data</span> and observations instead of explicitly programmed instructions

AI and ML <span style='color:#f7b731'>build models using the data</span> instead of starting with a model and testing it to see if it fits the predetermined models.

**Building a model**
1) Training Set
2) Testing Set
3) Validation Set (Improve accuracy, feedback)

It also <span style='color:#f7b731'>relies on statistics and mathematics </span>

**Model Techniques**
1) Artificial Intelligence (AI)
	 A technique which enables machines to mimic human behaviour
2) Machine Learning
	 Subset of AI technique which uses statistical methods to allow machines to improve with experience
3) Deep Learning
	 Subset of ML which makes the computation of multi-layer neural network feasible
### Statistics

Statistical models are designed for <mark class="hltr-orange">inference about the relationships between variables</mark>. Where <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Statistical modeling</span> is a formalization of relationships between variables in the data in the form of mathematical equations.

Statistics give you <span style='color:#f7b731'>logic, reasoning, and explicit answers</span>. It is applied to low dimension problems when you need to know more about data and the properties of estimators.

Examples of estimator properties: P-value, standard deviation, confidence interval or unbiased estimator

# Singularity
---
In terms of **maths**, <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>singularities</span> are places where mathematics "misbehave," typically by <span style='color:#f7b731'>generating infinitely large values</span>

Examples: $\frac{1}{x}$ where x goes to 0 

In terms of **technology**, <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>signularities</span> is a <span style='color:#f7b731'>hypothetical</span> point when technological growth becomes <span style='color:#f7b731'>uncontrollable and irreversible</span> which may result in <span style='color:#f7b731'>unforeseeable changes to human civilization</span>

Artificial intelligence (AI) transcends human intelligence, potentially erasing the boundary between humanity and computers.

Artificial Narrow Intelligence -> Artificial General Intelligence -> Artificial Superior Intelligence -> <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>Singularity</span>

This concept was coined by <span style='color:#2d98da'>Raymond Kurzweil</span>, where in 2045 it was predicted that AI will surpass human intelligence and will replace humans

90% expects the singularity will come, with <span style='color:#f7b731'>ground breaking inventions, better learning and quality of data will result in better AI, increasing the returns</span>

The conditions for reaching a singularity are <mark class="hltr-orange">practically difficult</mark>, humans should take control of the threat of AI development and utilization
- No AI for military purposes
- Humans should take care of AI threat
- Non profit research institutions (Open AI), shares and disclose discoveries related to AI and the risk of AI threat

# Innovation Areas of AI

## Perception

It refers to the act of <span style='color:#f7b731'>knowing or understanding something through our senses</span>

In terms of AI
1) Input Signal
2) Activation Function
3) Signal Propagation

Understanding the meaning of sound + recognizing types of situations <span style='color:#f7b731'>require comprehensive judgment skills</span>. Contextual understanding of conversation can be achieved through <span style='color:#f7b731'>natural language processing technology</span>

Examples:
- AI safety cameras, monitor driver and informs to take a break or to send alerts to him
## Prediction

It is the process of filling in missing information, by taking in information available. Knowing the <span style='color:#f7b731'>future is important especially in a competitive market</span>

Prediction -> AI judgement and review -> Humans

Process:
- Matching
- Finding Similar Uses
- Compare with similar group
- Generate prediction results

Examples;
- Google Translate
- Google email auto-completion (Based on email content, language patterns, typing history)
- Personalised Video Ranking (PVR), it is basically a video recommendation algorithm, using user watch history, comparing to similar group of users, it can generate recommendations
## Creation & Automation

AI can use content from existing data (Analyze and synthesize patterns) to then leverage this knowledge to <span style='color:#f7b731'>generate new and unique creative works</span> in various domains, such as art, music, or literature.

This is possible, if AI can <span style='color:#f7b731'>apply intricate relationships and structures</span> within input data

There are some <span style='color:#eb3b5a'>concerns</span> with AI generated art
1) Artists have uncertain futures as people can generate AI artworks for free
2) Ethics on AI generated art
3) Re-evaluation of rights granted to humans under copyright 

As for <span style='color:#0fb9b1'>automation</span>, in the early stages, AI scope of capabilities was limited and as it developed, it <span style='color:#f7b731'>can handle more complex tasks leading to automation</span>

<span style='color:#f7b731'>Communication and interaction is currently being automated</span> by AI, and its a question<span style='color:#f7b731'> if it can fully replace humans due to physiological resistance</span> especially when <span style='color:#f7b731'>dealing with human emotions</span>

Examples;
- AI-chatbots with better advanced natural language understanding, domain knowledge, and high level learning can carry out non-task-oriented conversations (Dialogs with no clear objective and variations of it)
-  Automated monk to give sermons
