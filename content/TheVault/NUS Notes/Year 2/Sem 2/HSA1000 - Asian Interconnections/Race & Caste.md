# Key Terms
---
1) **State**
A distinct **set of institutions** that has the authority to <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>make the rules which govern a society</span></b>.

The state itself is <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>powerful but they are not all powerful</span></b>.

2) **Caste**
A single term capable of expressing, organizing, and above all ‘systematizing’ India’s diverse forms of social identity, community, and organization.

![[Cast Exmple.png|center]]

3) **Race**
Categorizes people by <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>descent/genetic difference</span></b> (*but does it really?*).

<b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>Preoccupied with visible physical differences</span></b>. It came into widespread use as a result of European/Western imperialism.

> [!abstract] Race is a social construct
> There are genetic differences and <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>some traits are common in some communities</span></b> than others.
> 
> However this <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>does not mean that biological race exists</span></b>.

When we say someone is Chinese or Malay is <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>not always how they would see themselves</span></b> (*modern invention*). It could be that their <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>primary identity is more of their culture or religion</span></b> but reshaped and reformed into what we know today.

4) **Ethnicity**
Categorizes people by <b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-yellow)'>culture</span></b>.

<b><span style='color:var(--mk-color-red)'>Some degree of self-recognition</span></b>. It came into widespread use after second world war, preferred term in academia today.
